Modules in this Project
Patient & Doctor Management

Role-based accounts, profiles, department assignment, doctor availability & leave scheduling.

Appointment Booking

Patient self-booking with live doctor-availability validation, confirm/cancel/reschedule workflows.

ML No-Show Risk Prediction

Scikit-learn/XGBoost model scores every appointment Low/Medium/High risk automatically on save.

AI Chatbot Assistant

LangChain + Groq LLM with RAG (Hugging Face + FAISS) for doctor search, symptom routing, and hospital FAQs.

Medical Records & Prescriptions

Doctors document visits, prescribe medicines, and generate downloadable PDF prescriptions.

Billing & Payments

Auto-generated bills per appointment, partial payments, and PDF invoice downloads.

Email & SMS Notifications

Automated appointment confirmations and reminders via SMTP email and Twilio SMS.

Patient-Doctor Messaging

Direct in-app messaging between patients and their doctors.

Admin Console

Manage departments, doctors, patients, appointments, and view the Risk AI Engine dashboard.

Tech Stack
Python Django MySQL / SQLite Scikit-learn XGBoost LangChain Groq LLM Hugging Face FAISS Twilio Bootstrap 5
